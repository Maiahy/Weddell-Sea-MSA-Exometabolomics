Complete workflow used to generate the exometabolomics dataset presented in the paper, **“A Microbial Pathway for Methanesulfonic Acid Biosynthesis from DMSP Expands the Marine Sulfur Cycle”** by Anuj K. Chhalodia *et al.*

> **Context** Ice-associated systems, including ice-edge zones, melting continental ice, and icebergs, create dynamic environments that can substantially alter surrounding marine conditions. In particular, icebergs can strongly influence micronutrient availability along their drift path, and have been associated with changes in planktonic composition and abundance, increased biological activity, and enhanced carbon export. Here, we provide the code used to process and analyse an exometabolomics dataset of polar to semi-polar extracts of marine exometabolomes (i.e., dissolved marine organic compounds) originating from 13 sampling sites in the Weddell Sea, including an iceberg, sampled by the RV Tara during the Maria Klenova leg of the Mission Microbiomes Tara-AtlantECO expedition (2020-2022), between 25 January and 23 February 2022. We focused our analysis on five sulfur-containing marine metabolites, MSA, DMSP, DMSA, DMSOP, and gonyol, and analysed their relative abundances in the surface waters at each site to investigate how varying environmental and sea-ice conditions may influence the distribution of sulfur-containing metabolites in Antarctic waters.

The samples were preprocessed using Skyline Quantification Software (version 25.1.0.237), then filtered and analysed using in-house code (see the repository contents) in R (version 4.5.2).
